About me

I'm drawn to the gap between a demo and something you can trust.

I'm Ibu, a final-year B.Tech Information Technology student in Coimbatore, graduating in June 2027. I build AI agents and serverless apps on AWS, often in public and often against a weekend deadline.

My Supermarket agent is never allowed to invent a price: every number comes out of Postgres through a tool. This page holds my claims to the same rule. Each one links to the code, notebook or article behind it, and where there's no public proof, the receipt says so.

Writing these receipts caught three places where my own write-ups disagreed with my code. Those receipts print the corrected numbers.

Off the clock I'm into quantum computing. I organized an IBM Qiskit Fall Fest workshop on quantum algorithms.

How I work

  1. Ground the model. Facts come from data and tools, never from the model's memory.
  2. Publish the number that hurts. An R² of 0.94 means little without the 47.93% beside it.
  3. Own the loop. Fifty lines I can debug beat a framework I can't.
